Thank you for choosing LegaSea

Sandy floors. Salty air. Sunsets that stop you mid-sentence.

For years, LegaSea has been where our family's best memories are made — first steps in the surf, crab hunts by flashlight, and big dinners that run long into laughter you can hear down the beach. Now the house is yours, and we're so excited for you and your family to begin — or continue — building memories of your own here.

Below is your guide to everything you'll need to know for your stay. Initial each section as you read it and sign at the end — it takes about 3 minutes, gets your whole crew in the know, and helps make your stay safe and amazing. Your WiFi and arrival details unlock the moment you're done.

Section 2

Caring for the house

🛗Elevator: both doors — the handle door and the accordion door — must be fully closed in both the house and the garage for it to operate. Press the call button, wait for the red light before riding, and close the door after each use.
Please ride with a cell phone and don't let children ride alone. If the elevator ever gets stuck, the emergency release button is located up high, between the accordion and main door.
🚿Outdoor shower: please rinse all sand off before coming inside — it protects the home's plumbing from sand buildup. We appreciate your help keeping LegaSea in great shape!
💨Apartment dehumidifier: please leave it running for your whole stay. It keeps the space comfortable and protects against moisture.

Section 6 · Golf Cart Use Agreement

The golf cart 🛺

An electric golf cart is included with your stay — one of the best parts of LegaSea. Because it's a real vehicle on real roads, this section works like the rest of the agreement but with a checkbox after each part. Will anyone in your group drive it?

No problem — the terms below still apply if anyone changes their mind. Just come back to this page or text us at 704-616-7527 before using the cart.

Operation & Liability
·I will return the golf cart in the same condition as received, normal wear and tear accepted, and will operate it safely and responsibly.
·I agree to be legally and financially liable for all costs of repairs and any loss, damage, or injuries to my person, the cart, or the property and persons of others, regardless of fault — and to reimburse the owners for unrecovered costs, including deductibles and uninsured loss.
·I agree to hold harmless the owners for any and all damages and claims arising from use of the golf cart.
·I will inspect the cart before driving, report any concerns prior to use, and contact the owners immediately if I or any guest cause damage. If the cart becomes inoperable through no fault of mine, the owners will take reasonable steps to repair or replace it.
Charging the Electric Cart
·The cart is electric with lead-acid batteries. I will plug it in to charge when not in use — a full charge takes up to 8 hours. Keeping it charged is my responsibility; a $100 service fee applies if the cart becomes inoperable due to inadequate charging.
Caution: The garage door must remain open while charging. Lead-acid batteries produce hydrogen gas during charging — charging in an enclosed space is a fire and health hazard. Do not leave charging unattended.
Caution: Do not operate the cart during a rainstorm or through standing water. Doing so is an operating error and may result in a $200 fee for water damage.
State & Local Requirements
·Golf cart driving laws are the same as motor vehicle laws in North Carolina. I will follow all applicable laws of NC, Brunswick County, and the Town of Oak Island.
·The cart may only be driven on roads posted at 35 mph or below. It is not permitted to cross any bridge leaving Oak Island, and is not permitted on sidewalks or the beach.
·The cart may not be operated under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Open containers of alcohol are not permitted on the cart.
·Only listed authorized operators with a valid driver's license may drive the cart, and the cart will not carry more passengers than the number of seat belts available.
User Agreement
·I am responsible for all moving and/or parking violations issued while the cart is in my possession.
·I will not operate the cart on the beach, in sand or surf, or through standing water.
·At the beach front, the cart may only park in designated Town of Oak Island parking spaces. It is registered as an Oak Island resident vehicle and may use resident spots. There is no smoking on the cart.
·I agree to allow the owners to charge my credit card for any fees incurred in accordance with these terms, and will not dispute charges consistent with this agreement.
