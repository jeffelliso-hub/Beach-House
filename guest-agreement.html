Section 6 · Guest Agreement

The golf cart 🛺

An electric golf cart is included with your stay — one of the best ways to get to the beach and around the island. Here's everything drivers need to know.

Operation & Liability
I will return the cart in the same condition as received (normal wear and tear accepted) and operate it safely and responsibly.
I am legally and financially liable for all repair costs and any loss, damage, or injuries to my person, the cart, or the property and persons of others, regardless of fault — including deductibles and uninsured loss.
I hold the owners harmless for any damages or claims arising from use of the cart.
I will inspect the cart before driving, report concerns before use, and contact the owners immediately if damage occurs. If the cart becomes inoperable through no fault of mine, the owners will take reasonable steps to repair or replace it.
Charging the Electric Cart
The cart is electric with lead-acid batteries — plug it in when not in use. A full charge takes up to 8 hours. Keeping it charged is my responsibility; a $100 service fee applies if it becomes inoperable from inadequate charging.
Caution: The garage door must remain open while charging — lead-acid batteries produce hydrogen gas. Never charge in an enclosed space, and don't leave charging unattended.
Caution: Do not drive during a rainstorm or through standing water — this can cause a $200 water-damage fee.
Road Rules — NC & Oak Island
Golf cart laws are the same as motor vehicle laws in North Carolina. Follow all NC, Brunswick County, and Town of Oak Island rules.
Roads posted at 35 mph or below only. No crossing any bridge off the island, and no sidewalks, beach, sand, surf, or standing water.
No more passengers than seat belts.
Never drive under the influence — no open containers, no smoking on the cart.
At the beach front, park only in designated Town of Oak Island spaces. Tickets and violations are the guest's responsibility.
The cart is registered as an Oak Island resident vehicle and may use resident parking spots.
Will anyone in your group drive the cart?

No problem — if you change your mind during your stay, just come back to this page or text us at 704-616-7527.
