Operation & Liability

I will operate the cart safely and responsibly. I understand failure to comply with these terms may result in liability for damages and costs.
I agree to be legally and financially liable for all costs of repairs and any loss, damage, or injuries to my person, the cart, or the property and persons of others, regardless of fault.

I agree to hold harmless the owners for any and all damages and claims arising from use of the golf cart.
I agree to contact the owners immediately if I or any guest cause damage to the cart or others. I will inspect the cart before driving and report any concerns prior to use.
If the cart becomes inoperable through no fault of mine, the owners will take reasonable steps to repair or replace it.

Electric Cart Requirements

The cart is electric with lead-acid batteries. I will plug it in to charge when not in use. A full charge takes up to 8 hours.
Caution: The garage door must remain open while charging. Lead-acid batteries produce hydrogen gas during charging. Charging in an enclosed space is a fire and health hazard.
Keeping the cart charged is my responsibility. Failure to do so is an operating error. A $100 service fee applies if the cart becomes inoperable due to inadequate charging.
Caution: Do not operate the cart during a rainstorm or through standing water. Doing so is an operating error and may result in a $200 fee for water damage.

State & Local Requirements

I will operate the cart in accordance with all applicable laws in North Carolina, Brunswick County, and the Town of Oak Island.
Golf cart driving laws are the same as motor vehicle laws in North Carolina.
The cart may only be driven on roads posted at 35 mph or below. It is not permitted to cross any bridge leaving Oak Island, and is not permitted on sidewalks or the beach.
The cart may not be operated under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Open containers of alcohol are not permitted on the cart.
Only listed authorized operators with a valid driver’s license may drive the cart.
The cart will not carry more passengers than the number of seat belts available.

User Agreement

I am responsible for all moving and/or parking violations issued while the cart is in my possession.
I will not operate the cart on the beach, in sand or surf, or through standing water.
At the beach front, the cart may only park in designated Town of Oak Island parking spaces. Parking in non-designated areas may result in a citation for which I will be solely responsible.
There is no smoking permitted on the cart.
I agree to allow the owners to charge my credit card for any fees incurred in accordance with these terms, and will not dispute charges consistent with this agreement.
